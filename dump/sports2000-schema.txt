Sports2000 schema overview (based on dump/sports2000.df)

The schema defines sequences and tables for the Sports2000 sample database. The first 500 lines include sequence definitions and the beginning of the table/field definitions.

Visible tables in the excerpt:
- Benefits: employee benefit information, keyed by EmpNum.
- BillTo: billing addresses keyed by CustNum and BillToID.
- Bin: warehouse/item bin inventory, with BinNum as its primary key and additional indexes for ItemNum and WarehouseNum/ItemNum.
- Customer: customer identity and address fields, including CustNum, Name, Address, City, State, Country, Phone, Contact, SalesRep, Comments, CreditLimit, Balance, Terms, Discount, PostalCode, and Fax.

The excerpt also defines sequences such as NextCustNum, NextInvNum, NextOrdNum, NextItemNum, NextRefNum, and NextVisitor. Use the complete `dump/sports2000.df` from the source project as the authoritative schema when building the database.
